🔗 How to Play Mahjong Connect

1
Match Identical Tiles: Tap two matching tiles with the same symbol and character.
2
Max 2 Turns Rule: The connecting line between the two tiles cannot make more than two 90-degree turns.
3
Clear The Board: Connect and remove all pairs before the countdown timer expires!
