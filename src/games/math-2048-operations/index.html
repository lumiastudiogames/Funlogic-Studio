How to Play Math 2048

• Slide: Swipe, use Arrow keys, or tap the directional buttons.

• ➕ Addition Mode (Default): When two matching numbers collide, they sum together:
  2 + 2 = 4
  4 + 4 = 8
  8 + 8 = 16
  16 + 16 = 32... all the way to 2048!

• ✖️ Double Mode (×2): Multiplies each matching number by 2 (2×2=4, 4×2=8, 8×2=16...).

• Reach the golden 2048 tile to win!
